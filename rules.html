Rules & Info

=== GAME RULES ===

Objective: Compete in teams to earn the most points by finding QR code challenge nodes around Sydney, completing challenges, and banking your points at Central Station before each round ends.

How It Works

  • 3 teams compete across 2 rounds with a 90-minute lunch break between them.
  • Each round starts at Central Station, which is also the home base and safe zone.
  • Challenge locations appear as nodes on the Life360 map.
  • Visit a node, scan its QR code, and complete the challenge to earn points.

Scoring

Challenges award rubber bands worth:

  • 🟢 Green = 2 points
  • 🔵 Blue = 5 points
  • 🔴 Red = 10 points

Wear earned bands on your right forearm until they are banked at Central Station.

Banking Points

  • Return to Central Station to bank your bands.
  • Once banked, points cannot be stolen.
  • Only banked points count towards your team's score.
  • Unbanked bands at the end of a round score 0 points.

Tagging

  • Players can be tagged anywhere except inside Central Station.
  • You must have at least 1 unbanked band to initiate a tag.
  • The player carrying fewer bands becomes the tagger.
  • If both players carry the same number, play best-of-three Rock Paper Scissors.
  • The tagger steals bands, up to the 20-band carrying limit.
  • The tagged player must complete 40 star jumps before resuming play.

Carrying Bands

  • Maximum of 20 unbanked bands on your arm.
  • You may give unbanked bands to teammates.
  • Equivalent bands may be exchanged (e.g. 2 blue = 1 red).

Challenges

  • You cannot rescan a QR code until you complete its challenge or finish an 8-minute veto.
  • Failing or skipping a challenge starts an 8-minute veto.
  • During a veto, you cannot attempt challenges but may still move and tag.
  • Timed challenges continue even if you are tagged.

Playing as a Pair

  • Pairs act as a single player.
  • Wear your ID band on your head.
  • If one player is tagged, both players are tagged.
  • Only one player carries the team's bands.
  • Success or failure applies to the pair.

Rounds

Round 1

  • Large play area across inner Sydney.
  • Encourages use of trains, metro, ferries and light rail.

Round 2

  • Smaller CBD-focused play area.
  • Greater emphasis on foot chases, tagging and strategy.

Safety

  • Keep your armband visible.
  • Leave Life360 location sharing on.
  • Use the WhatsApp group only for emergencies during rounds.
  • Do not interfere with transport staff or security.
  • Do not film strangers without permission.
  • If approached by police or security, contact the organiser immediately.
  • Use common sense and respect others.

What to Bring

  • Opal card or payment method.
  • Fully charged portable power bank.
  • A bag or clothing with two pockets large enough to hold a lemon.

Winning

The team with the highest total of banked points across both rounds wins the game.

You can access this page at any time for reference.
